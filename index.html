Meet your crew.

Captain hats optional. Great music guaranteed.

Set sail on a voyage of velvety vocals and sultry sax solos. The Yacht Rock-ettes are the Midwest’s premier yacht rock cover band, bringing you the smoothest hits of the ’70s and early ’80s.

From Michael McDonald’s unmistakable growl to the soulful stylings of Sade, these ladies have the chops to take you straight back. Sway to Hall & Oates classics like “She’s Gone” and “Rich Girl,” bask in the sunny sounds of Christopher Cross and Kenny Loggins, or jam out to Toto’s guitar riffs. These girls put the ROCK in “smooth rock.”

Based in Chicago, the Yacht Rock-ettes play corporate events, private celebrations, festivals, and theatres across the Midwest. Buttery harmonies. Impeccable musicianship. Sophisticated soft rock at its finest.

What we bring

  • Songs everyone knows

    The set draws on the yacht rock and soft rock hits that defined an era, from “What a Fool Believes” to “Rich Girl.” See the full song list, and tell us if there is a song you would love to hear at a private event.

  • A full live band

    A female-fronted lineup with strong lead vocals and rich harmonies, backed by a full live band. Available for festivals, theatres, corporate events and private celebrations across the Midwest.

  • Acoustic sets

    For cocktail hours, smaller rooms and intimate private events, the band is also available for acoustic sets: the same songs and harmonies, stripped down. Mention the acoustic set when you send an inquiry, and we can share video on request.

  • Ready for your stage

    Planning a festival, a theatre date or a corporate event? We make it easy on your producer, with clear tech and logistics information up front. A press kit, stage plot and video are available on request.
